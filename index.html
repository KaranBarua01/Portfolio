Approach

How I build

Operations first

I start from the real workflow: handoffs, failure points, approvals, duplicate work, timing rules, and the information people actually need.

Make it observable

I prefer systems with clear IDs, status tracking, auditability, health checks, and explicit rules instead of hidden manual logic.

Automate carefully

I separate ingestion, validation, transformation, persistence, and user-facing actions so failures are easier to isolate and recover from.

Protect sensitive data

Credentials, customer information, and proprietary company logic do not belong in public repositories or screenshots.
